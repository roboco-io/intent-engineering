01 — The Problem

Vibe coding made code generation instant. But without clear intent, AI produces 337 lines of tech specs when you needed 47 lines of direction.

Without Intent

  • AI generates tech stack recommendations
  • AI writes 7-week timelines
  • AI creates user stories, glossaries, sign-off sheets
  • 8 files, 3,740 lines, 132KB of noise
  • You still don't know what to build

With Intent

  • Why: one paragraph on why this exists
  • What: concrete features, no how
  • Not: explicit boundaries AI won't cross
  • 1 file, 47 lines, clarity
  • AI builds exactly what you meant

02 — The Framework

An Intent Document has four sections. Nothing more.

Why

Why does this exist?

The problem, the goal, the measure of success. Changes here mean a pivot. In explore mode, this is a hypothesis. In clarified mode, this is conviction.

What

What are we building?

Features, user flows, edge cases. Concrete but never prescriptive about implementation. No tech stack, no architecture, no timeline. Those are AI's job.

Not

What will we never do?

Hard boundaries AI must not cross. Security constraints, scope limits, quality bars, forbidden patterns. This is the guardrail that makes autonomous AI safe.

Learnings

What did we discover?

A living log of experiments, interviews, prototypes, and their outcomes. Each entry has a date, what was tried, what was learned, and what's next. This is how intent evolves.

05 — Principles

Four rules that make Intent Engineering work.

Never write How

The moment you specify implementation, you're doing traditional engineering. Write "no external cache layer" instead of "use Redis." Constrain the boundaries, not the path.

Admit uncertainty

Mark unknowns with (?). An honest "I don't know yet" is more valuable than a confident guess. Intent clarifies through exploration, not imagination.

Kill fast

If exploration shows the idea is dead, record why and move on. A well-documented killed intent saves future you from repeating the same mistake.

Intent precision = output quality

"Build a good app" is not intent. "New users experience first value in 30 seconds" is. The sharper your intent, the better the AI performs.
